## 1. PenRX+ is NOT a diagnostic or treatment tool
**ليست أداة تشخيص أو علاج.** PenRX+ is a documentation and drafting aid. It does not diagnose disease, does not decide therapy, does not triage, and is **not a medical device** under Egyptian (EDA) or EU (MDR) regulation. It must never be used as the basis for a clinical decision.

## 2. The doctor is solely responsible
**المسؤولية الكاملة على الطبيب.** Every prescription produced with PenRX+ is **100% the prescribing doctor's** medical, professional, syndicate and legal responsibility — including drug choice, indication, dose, route, frequency, duration, interactions, allergies, pregnancy/lactation, renal/hepatic adjustment, paediatric and geriatric dosing, controlled-substance rules and patient counselling. Nothing in the app constitutes medical advice, a second opinion, or a recommendation.

## 3. The drug catalogue is reference data, not an authority
The bundled catalogue contains **43,713 records** (brand names, active ingredients, forms, strengths, companies) assembled from public references and from contributions saved by doctors. It may be **incomplete, outdated, mistranslated or wrong**:
* formularies and available strengths in Egypt change without notice;
* community-contributed entries are **not independently verified** by a clinician before publication;
* dose suggestions and the smart-dose engine are algorithmic guesses from the stored fields — **always confirm against the current package leaflet / Egyptian National Drug Authority reference**.

## 4. AI and interaction features are advisory only
The interaction checker, the AI drug-synthesis suggestion and the AI prescription review are generated by a language model or by heuristics. They produce **no alert** when they fail to recognise an entry, and they can miss real interactions. Absence of a warning means nothing. Never accept or reject a therapy because of an AI output.

## 5. Do not use it in emergencies
In an emergency, poisoning, overdose or anaphylaxis: contact emergency services and your poison-control centre. Do not consult this app.

## 6. Patient-facing use is prohibited
Patients, pharmacists and non-prescribing staff must not use this software to self-medicate or to substitute for a professional prescription.

If you find a wrong drug entry, dose field or missing interaction, send the exact name and the correct value with the source to **`<support@your-domain>`**. Corrections are pushed in the next catalogue update — but the correction is not a substitute for your own verification.
