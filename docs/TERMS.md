## 3. Payment (الدفع)
Payment is manual, in Egyptian Pounds, via **Vodafone Cash** or **InstaPay** to the wallet number shown on the official download page, followed by the WhatsApp confirmation flow in the app.
* Activation happens after our admin verifies the transfer — normally within a few working hours; a pending request is visible in the app.
* The trial does not require payment and does not auto-renew.
* No money is collected inside the app; **we never ask for bank credentials, PINs or card numbers.**

## 4. Refunds (الاسترداد)
Because the licence is activated instantly, fees paid for an activated period are **non-refundable**, except: (a) a duplicate or mistaken transfer reported within 7 days, or (b) a proven technical defect we cannot fix within 14 days. Approved refunds are sent back through the same Vodafone Cash / InstaPay number.

## 5. Offline grace period (مدة السماح عند انقطاع الإنترنت)
Licence verification is online. If the device loses connectivity, the app keeps working for **up to 3 days (72 hours) of continuous offline time** using the signed offline token.
* After 72 hours the app asks to reconnect; **no data is deleted and the clinic records stay fully readable and exportable**.
* Print, export and encrypted backup remain available offline so prescriptions are never blocked; only new cloud sync waits for connectivity.
